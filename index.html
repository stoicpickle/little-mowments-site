Little Mowments

A little time in the garden.

Little Mowments is a quiet lawn-mowing game. Spend an afternoon across eight yards at four homes. Choose a property from Yards, mow its front and back lawns, and come back to where you left off. Change the light and enjoy gentle music and recorded mower sounds.

Support and feedback

For help, questions or feedback, email russwonsley@gmail.com.

If something goes wrong, please include your device model, the app version and a short description of what happened. A screenshot can help. Please avoid including sensitive personal information.

If you are testing with TestFlight, you can also send feedback through the TestFlight app.

Your progress

Your mowing progress and sound, visual and accessibility settings save on your device. New afternoon grows back every lawn across all four properties and clears where you left off; your settings stay. Deleting the app removes its local data from that device.
