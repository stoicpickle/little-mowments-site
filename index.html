Little Mowments

A little time in the garden.

Little Mowments is a quiet lawn-mowing game. Follow your own paths through three gardens, change the light, and enjoy gentle music and recorded mower sounds.

Support and feedback

For help, questions or feedback, email russwonsley@gmail.com.

If something goes wrong, please include your device model, the app version and a short description of what happened. A screenshot can help. Please avoid including sensitive personal information.

If you are testing with TestFlight, you can also send feedback through the TestFlight app.

Your progress

The game saves your progress and settings on your device. New afternoon asks for confirmation before resetting mowing progress across all three gardens. Deleting the app removes its local data from that device.
