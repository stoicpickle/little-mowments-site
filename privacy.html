Little Mowments · Support

Privacy policy

Updated October 24, 2026

For Little Mowments privacy questions or feedback, contact hello@littlemowments.com.

Information stored by the game

Little Mowments saves your mowing progress and sound, visual and accessibility settings on your device so you can continue later. The game does not require an account, and this information is not sent to a developer-operated server. The current game has no ads, tracking, third-party analytics or in-app purchases.

The game does not request access to your microphone, camera, photos, contacts or location. Its mower sounds are prerecorded assets; it does not record you.

Feedback and TestFlight

If you email feedback, the developer receives the information you include, such as your email address, message, device details and attachments. It is used to respond and improve the game. Avoid sending information you do not want included in feedback.

Feedback is kept while it is needed to investigate your report or improve the game, then deleted when no longer needed for those purposes. You may request earlier deletion by emailing the address above. This applies to feedback sent directly to the developer.

If you install or test the game through Apple TestFlight, Apple may process information about your beta use and feedback under Apple's own terms and privacy practices. Apple may also provide beta reports to the developer. This is separate from the game's local save data.

Your choices

New afternoon grows back all eight lawns across four properties and clears mowing progress and where you left off. Your sound, visual and accessibility settings stay. Deleting the app removes its local data from that device. Device backups may retain data under your Apple backup settings. You can ask the developer to delete feedback you sent directly.

External links

The privacy, support and engine-license links open external websites only when you tap them. External websites handle information according to their own privacy practices.

This website

These support pages contain no cookies, analytics, forms or scripts. They are hosted on GitHub Pages; the hosting provider may process technical information needed to serve the pages under its own privacy practices.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the game's data practices change. The current game has no cloud account, ads, tracking, third-party analytics or in-app purchases.
