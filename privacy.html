Little Mowments · Support

Privacy policy

Updated October 21, 2026

Little Mowments is developed by Russell Wonsley. Questions about privacy or the beta can be sent to russwonsley@gmail.com.

Information stored by the game

Little Mowments stores mowing progress, garden state and preferences on your device so you can continue playing later. The current preview does not require an account and does not send this information to a developer-operated server. It has no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics service.

The game does not request access to your microphone, camera, photos, contacts or location. Its mower sounds are prerecorded assets; it does not record you.

Feedback and TestFlight

If you choose to email feedback, the developer receives the information you include, such as your email address, message, device details and attachments. This information is used to respond to you and improve the game. Avoid sending information you do not want included in feedback.

Feedback is retained while needed to investigate the report or improve this preview, then deleted when no longer needed for those purposes. You may request earlier deletion by emailing the address above. This retention policy applies to feedback received directly by the developer; Apple manages information held by TestFlight under its own policies.

TestFlight is operated by Apple. If you install or test this preview through TestFlight, Apple may process usage, crash and feedback information under its own terms and privacy practices and provide beta reports to the developer. This is separate from the game's local save data.

Your choices

New afternoon resets mowing progress for all three gardens after confirmation; it does not erase your separate sound and visual preferences. Deleting the app removes its local data from that device. Device backups may retain data under your Apple backup settings. You can contact the developer to request deletion of feedback you sent directly.

External links

The privacy, support and engine-license links open external websites only when you tap them. External websites handle information according to their own privacy practices.

This website

These support pages contain no cookies, analytics, forms or scripts. They are hosted on GitHub Pages; the hosting provider may process technical information needed to serve the pages under its own privacy practices.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the game's data practices change. The preview currently has no cloud account, advertising, paid purchases or developer-run analytics.
